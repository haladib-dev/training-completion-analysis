Training Completion Analysis & Classification
About this project

This project is based on real organisational data and shows how I worked with employee training records to analyse completion and support structured decision-making.

The goal was to understand training participation and outcomes, and to organise the data in a way that makes it easy to analyse and interpret.

What the project does
Cleans and organises employee training data
Analyses training completion across different groups
Applies a simple classification model based on scores
Generates insights to support monitoring and decision-making
Data

The dataset includes employee-level information such as:

Field / Area / Section
Grade
Training score
Participation details

This reflects the type of structured organisational data I worked with in practice.

How it works

1. Data preparation
The data is organised and cleaned to ensure consistency across fields.

2. Classification
Employees are grouped based on their training score:

High (80 and above)
Medium (50–79)
Low (below 50)

This creates a simple and transparent classification model.

3. Completion indicator
A binary variable (Yes/No) is used to identify whether the training was successfully completed.

4. Analysis
The data is analysed to identify patterns, for example:

Completion rates by area or section
Differences across grades
Distribution of performance levels
